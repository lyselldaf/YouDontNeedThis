# You Don't Need This

Your bags are full. Again. Somewhere in there is a Mysterious Glowing Flask, three Tattered Letters, a Bundle of Gnoll Teeth and something called "Gooey Spider Leg" that you've carried since level 12. Are they important? Will throwing them out break a quest chain you've forgotten about?

And your quest log? Full of grey quests from three zones ago that you're never going back for.

**You Don't Need This** knows. Once every quest that uses an item is done, it gives that item a big red X and says, gently but firmly: *you don't need this.* And when your quest log is clogged with low-level leftovers, it lines them up so you can clear them out in a few clicks.

## Clean out your bags

- **Red X marks the junk.** Items you're done with get a red tint and a red X right in your bags and bank. One glance, and you know what can go.
- **Gives you a nudge.** Log in and get a quick count of how much clutter you're hauling around. Turn in a quest and its leftovers get called out right away.
- **Tooltips that explain themselves.** Hover a quest item to see either the quest you already finished with it, or the quest that still wants it.
- **Thinks ahead.** Picked up a quest item before you found its quest? It won't be flagged by mistake. The addon already knows thousands of quest items in WoW Forever.
- **You get the final say.** Know better? `/ydnt ignore <item>` marks an item as junk, `/ydnt keep <item>` makes sure it's never marked.

## Clean out your quest log

- **Every quest you've outgrown, in one window.** Grey quests by default, or grey and green, or anything a set number of levels below you. Your pick.
- **Abandon with one click.** Each quest has its own Abandon button, and the addon asks before it does anything. Or hit "Abandon all" — quests that are ready to turn in are left alone, because free XP is still free XP.
- **The items go with them.** Abandon a quest and the items it needed get the red X too. Change your mind and pick it up again, and they count again.
- **Always within reach.** Open it from the minimap button, your own key binding, the button on the quest log, or `/ydnt quests`. After you log in or level up, a chat line tells you how many quests just went grey, with a **[Show]** link.

## Hands off your stuff

It never deletes, sells or moves anything, and it never abandons a quest unless you click. Whether to vendor that flask or keep the letter for sentimental reasons is up to you.

## Just install it

No setup and no commands needed. It works with the default bags, **Bagnon**, **Baganator**, **AdiBags** (with its own "You Don't Need This" section) and **ArkInventory**, and Questie is **not** required.

Want to tweak it? Turn the red marks, alerts, reminders and minimap button on or off in Esc > Options > AddOns, or click You Don't Need This in the addons button by the minimap. Type `/ydnt` to list everything in your bags you can toss, or `/ydnt help` for every command.

If someone in your guild or group runs a newer version, you'll get a heads-up in chat to update.

Found something flagged that shouldn't be? Run `/ydnt why` on it and drop the output in the comments.

Playing in another language? Quest names from the built-in database show in English; quests you've had in your quest log show in your language.
